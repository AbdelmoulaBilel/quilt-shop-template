A little shop full of colour, thread and good company

Tucked away on a quiet country road, our shop has been called an Aladdin’s cave for makers. Shelves are packed with fabrics, notions and everything you need to create beautiful quilts and stitched projects.

It is also the heart of a community: every week people of all ages come to sew, learn and chat — from total beginners to seasoned quilters. Visitors from all over the world are welcome, and we happily host tour groups.

  • Hundreds of fabrics & threads
  • Classes for every level
  • On-location classes for groups
  • Worldwide mail order
